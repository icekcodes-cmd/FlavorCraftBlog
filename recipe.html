Handmade Truffle & Wild Mushroom Tagliatelle

Learn how to knead silky fresh egg pasta from scratch and pair it with a rich garlic, parmesan, and black truffle reduction.

⏱ Prep: 45 mins 🔥 Cook: 15 mins 🍽 Servings: 4 People ⭐ Skill: Intermediate

Ingredients

For the Fresh Pasta:

  • 2 cups "00" Italian Flour (plus extra for dusting)
  • 4 large pastured egg yolks
  • 2 whole farm-fresh eggs
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 pinch fine sea salt

For the Truffle Sauce:

  • 3 tbsp unsalted European butter
  • 2 cups mixed wild mushrooms (chanterelles or cremini), sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano, freshly grated
  • 1 tbsp black truffle paste or fresh truffle shavings
  • Fresh flat-leaf parsley for garnish

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1. Make the Pasta Dough

    Mound the flour on a clean wooden board and create a well in the center. Add the eggs, egg yolks, olive oil, and salt. Gently beat the eggs with a fork, slowly incorporating flour from the inner rim until a shaggy dough forms.

  2. 2. Knead and Rest

    Knead vigorously with the palms of your hands for 8 to 10 minutes until the dough becomes elastic and smooth. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and let it rest at room temperature for 30 minutes.

  3. 3. Roll and Cut Tagliatelle

    Roll the dough out thin on a floured surface using a rolling pin or pasta machine. Fold loosely into thirds and slice into long, 1/4-inch ribbons (tagliatelle). Dust with flour to prevent sticking.

  4. 4. Sauté the Mushrooms

    Melt butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the sliced wild mushrooms and sauté until golden brown and caramelized (about 5 minutes). Stir in the minced garlic for the final 60 seconds.

  5. 5. Boil and Combine

    Drop the fresh pasta into a large pot of boiling salted water. Fresh pasta cooks quickly—only 2 to 3 minutes! Transfer pasta directly into the mushroom skillet using tongs, add heavy cream, parmesan, and truffle paste, tossing vigorously until glossy.
